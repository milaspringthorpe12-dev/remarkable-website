Services

What RE:Markable Offers

Five ways of working together, built to be combined or taken one at a time, whatever your brand actually needs right now.

01

Branding

Your brand identity: logo, colour, type, tone, built so it actually looks like a business people trust and remember. This isn't a logo hand-off; it's the visual and verbal foundation everything else gets built on.

  • Visual identity, logo, colour palette, typography
  • Brand voice & messaging guidelines
  • Brand foundations & positioning
  • Templates for consistent day-to-day use
02

Content Strategy

The plan behind the posts. Content pillars, messaging direction, and a clear point of view, so your content builds something over time instead of just filling a grid.

  • Content pillars mapped to your audience
  • Editorial calendars & content planning
  • Campaign concepts & messaging
  • Performance review & strategy refinement
03

Digital Marketing

Joined-up campaigns across the channels that matter, from social media to paid and organic growth, so your brand shows up with strategy behind every move, not just activity for the sake of it.

  • Content creation & scheduling
  • Community management
  • Platform-specific strategy (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)
  • Monthly reporting & insights
04

Email Marketing

Newsletters and campaigns that turn subscribers into customers, written with the same voice and care as everything else your brand sends out, and built to actually get opened.

  • Email strategy & audience segmentation
  • Newsletter design & copywriting
  • Automated flows (welcome, nurture, re-engagement)
  • Performance tracking & optimisation
05

Market Research

A clear-eyed look at your audience, competitors and category, so every strategy decision is grounded in what your market actually wants, not just a guess.

  • Audience & customer insights
  • Competitor analysis
  • Category & trend research
  • Findings translated into strategy
